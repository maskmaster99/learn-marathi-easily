Meet The Founder

Namaskar! I am Shruti Bandekar, the founder of Learn Marathi Easily. I hold a Master’s degree in Marathi Literature (M.A. in Marathi) and have a passion for teaching the Marathi language to people from different linguistic backgrounds.

I started my YouTube channel, Learn Marathi Easily, in 2020 with a mission to make learning Marathi simple, enjoyable, and accessible to everyone. My aim is to help Hindi- and English-speaking learners understand Marathi, improve their language skills, and communicate confidently in everyday situations.

Through my lessons, I focus on practical vocabulary, grammar, sentence formation, everyday conversations, and the correct usage of Marathi expressions. I believe that learning a new language should be an engaging and rewarding experience.

To make learning Marathi more accessible, I also share educational content through my Instagram page, WhatsApp Channel, Telegram Channel and Facebook, where learners can discover useful words, sentences, grammar tips, and learning resources.

At Learn Marathi Easily, my goal is to help you progress from the basics to more advanced Marathi at your own pace.

Whether you are learning Marathi for daily communication, work, friendships, or personal growth, I am here to make your learning journey easier and more enjoyable.

Thank you for being a part of the Learn Marathi Easily community. Let’s learn Marathi together!

— Shruti BandekarFounder, Learn Marathi Easily
